Key Insights

📈 Overall Business Performance
Total Sales reached ₹75.21M from 1,000 orders.
Total Profit generated was ₹15.04M, indicating healthy profitability.
A total of 3,097 units were sold across all product categories.

🌍 Regional Performance
North Region recorded the highest sales (₹20.3M).
East Region generated the lowest sales (₹16.7M).
Sales performance is concentrated in North and South regions.

🛍️ Category Performance
Electronics was the top-performing category with ₹17.3M in sales.
Home & Kitchen generated the lowest sales among major categories (₹13.3M).
Electronics and Clothing together contributed a significant share of total revenue.

📅 Monthly Sales Trends
July recorded the highest monthly sales (₹7.6M).
June recorded the lowest monthly sales (₹5.0M).
Sales fluctuated throughout the year, showing seasonal demand patterns.

💰 Profit Trends
July also generated the highest monthly profit (₹1.53M).
June recorded the lowest monthly profit (₹1.00M).
Higher sales months generally corresponded with higher profitability.

⭐ Customer Experience
Average customer rating is 3.01/5, indicating moderate customer satisfaction.
There is scope to improve customer experience and product quality.

Despite significant discounting, the business maintained strong profitability of ₹15.04M.
